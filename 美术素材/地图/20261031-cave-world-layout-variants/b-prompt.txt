VARIANT B — ASYMMETRIC CHAMBER CLUSTERS.
Exact title: “方案 B · 矿厅簇群”

Follow the guide's 14-node tree: three identifiable MEDIUM cave halls arranged left-middle, upper-right and lower-middle, connected by narrow winding necks. Each hall has several different short side-pocket destinations. Upper-right hall connects only to the first hall and its own pockets; do NOT reconnect it to the lower hall. All local arms end in rock. The three halls are orientation landmarks and places that concentrate different content choices, not three huge empty oval arenas.

Make hall silhouettes distinct: the first an off-center low arch with rubble and several exits; upper-right a layered slanted shelf with a pinched ceiling and elevated side pocket; lower hall a deep concave working bowl with a heavy roof and lower mineral alcoves. Hall clearance about four to six tiny character heights, ordinary necks about two. No large central pillar creating a traversal loop. Small optional wall shelves are acceptable. Keep halls in the same 2D side view.
